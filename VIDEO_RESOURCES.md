## Financial education videos

The `/videos` page links to three Reserve Bank of India financial-literacy films currently linked from RBI’s official [Financial Education Films page](https://rbi.org.in/FinancialEducation/Films.aspx):

- Credit from formal sources (Hindi with English subtitles), YouTube ID `RMiTaO_Z7b0`.
- Responsible borrowing (Hindi with English subtitles), YouTube ID `UqBGxIFKSDE`.
- Timely repayment (Hindi with English subtitles), YouTube ID `y1_bJ_I2XMA`.

The page uses click-to-load `youtube-nocookie.com` embeds and provides a direct YouTube link. Video content remains hosted and controlled by its publisher. RBI does not endorse Sahu Enterprises or its services. Selecting a video connects the visitor to third-party YouTube content.
