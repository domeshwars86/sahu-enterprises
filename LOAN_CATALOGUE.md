# Loan catalogue

The application reads public product cards from `config/loan_catalogue.php`. Only entries with `enabled: true` are sent to the browser.

## Planned, not offered or advertised as active

The config groups personal/retail, home/property, business/MSME, other transport, secured/specialized and agriculture/rural finance categories as planned research only. They are not active products and no lender partnerships or availability are claimed. Confirm actual DSA appointments, geography and written product permission before activation.

Sahu Enterprises is a loan assistance/DSA business, not the lender. A regulated lending institution decides eligibility, terms, pricing, approval and disbursement.
